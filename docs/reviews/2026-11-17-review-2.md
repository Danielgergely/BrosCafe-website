## Design verdict

It meets most of the brief: the palette is soft and warm, every picture is the real place, the tab bar and header are identical on every page and size, the two new products are in the tab bar, the hero chip, their own scene and the hub without pushing coffee out of the lead, and tap-for-info works well (cups, hours, loyalty, menu sheet). The collage look — tilted frames, stickers, washes — is distinctive enough that it does not read as a template.

What is still "standard website": the subpages are stacked sections with a hero, cards and a one-screen footer; the home page's sixth scene is a sitemap; and the scroll motion is the common "sections slide in and parts fade up" pattern rather than something you would remember. It is also not "little scrolling" yet: seven screens on the home page, six to eight on the two product pages.

Highest-impact single change: cut the home page to four scenes and spend the saved space on one scrubbed centrepiece — for example the pour film or the eight cups pinned and driven by the scroll, ending on the two product cards. That fixes the length, gives the client the scroll moment they asked for, and removes the short-phone clipping (B2, S4) at the same time because fewer things have to fit per screen.
